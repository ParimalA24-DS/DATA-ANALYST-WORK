# 📊 **Bank Loan & Stakeholder Insights Dashboard**
### **Enhancing Financial Decision-Making with Data**

---

## **📌 1. Project Overview**  
### **🚀 Problem Statement:**  
Banks struggle to analyze **loan applications, premium trends, and stakeholder performance** efficiently.  

### **💡 Solution:**  
We built an **interactive Tableau dashboard** to:  
✅ Track **Premium Collections Over Time**  
✅ Analyze **Insurer Performance & Profitability**  
✅ Compare **Cost Ratios Across Regions**

## **📌 2. Business Problem & Solution**  
| **Challenges Faced** | **Solution in Tableau** |
|----------------------|------------------------|
| No real-time premium tracking | 📊 Created **Trend Line Charts** for premium analysis |
| Insurer performance was unclear | 📍 Used **Bar Charts & KPIs** for insurer ranking |
| No demographic insights | 🏷️ Added **Filters for Gender, Age, and City** |

📉 **Before:** Manual reports delayed business decisions  
📈 **After:** Stakeholders access **real-time insights via Tableau Server**
