# 🚗 Car Sales Dashboard 📊  

### 📌 Project Overview  
This project presents a **dynamic and interactive Car Sales Dashboard in Tableau**, analyzing car dealership sales performance. The dashboard provides valuable **insights and KPIs** such as **total sales, YOY growth, average price, and regional performance**, helping **ABC Company** make **data-driven decisions**.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)  

| **KPI**                      | **Description**  |
|------------------------------|----------------|
| 📌 **YTD Total Sales**         | Total sales from start of the year |
| 🔄 **YOY Sales Growth**       | Percentage change in sales vs. last year |
| 💰 **YTD Average Price**      | Average price of cars sold this year |
| 🚗 **YTD Cars Sold**          | Total number of cars sold YTD |
| 📊 **YOY Growth in Cars Sold** | Growth rate in number of cars sold |

**Formula for YOY Growth:**  
\[
{YOY Growth} = \frac{\text{YTD Sales} - \text{PYTD Sales}}{\text{PYTD Sales}}
\]

## 📊 Dashboard Components  

1️⃣ **YTD Weekly Sales Trend** 📈 *(Line Chart)*  
   - Tracks **weekly performance of YTD sales** over time.  

2️⃣ **YTD Total Sales by Body Style** 🚙 *(Pie Chart)*  
   - Identifies the **best-selling car types** (SUV, Sedan, Hatchback, etc.).  

3️⃣ **YTD Total Sales by Color** 🎨 *(Donut Chart)*  
   - Analyzes which **car colors contribute most to total sales**.  

4️⃣ **YTD Cars Sold by Dealer Region** 🏙 *(Bar Chart)*  
   - Shows **sales distribution across different dealership regions**.  

5️⃣ **Company-Wise Sales Trend** 🏢 *(Tabular Grid)*  
   - Displays **sales trends for different car brands**.
