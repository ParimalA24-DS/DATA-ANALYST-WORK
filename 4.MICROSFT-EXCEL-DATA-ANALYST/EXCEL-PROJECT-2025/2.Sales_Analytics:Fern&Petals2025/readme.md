# Ferns & Petals Sales Dashboard Project | Excel Analytics 
A real-time interactive Excel dashboard that uncovers key business insights for India's top gifting brand.

![Ferns & Petals Sales Dashboard](https://github.com/ParimalA24-DS/DATA-ANALYST-WORK/blob/main/4.MICROSFT-EXCEL-DATA-ANALYST/EXCEL-PROJECT-2025/2.Sales_Analytics%3AFern%26Petals2025/FERN%26PETALPROJECTFILES/FNP_SALESDB2025.PNG?raw=true)

## 📌 About the Company  
**Ferns & Petals** is one of India’s top gifting companies.  
They deliver flowers, cakes, and gifts across India during festivals like **Diwali**, **Valentine’s Day**, and **Raksha Bandhan**.

---

## ❓ Problem Statement  
- The company had **huge Excel files** with raw data  
- But no clear insights:  
  - Which product sells most?  
  - Which city performs best?  
  - How do sales vary during festivals?

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used  
✅ Power Query – Clean & shape data  ✅ Power Pivot – Build relationships  
✅ Pivot Tables – For summary metrics  ✅ Pivot Charts – For visual trends  
✅ Slicers – For interactivity
---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning (Power Query)  
- Removed blanks & spelling mistakes  
- Added calculated columns:  
  - `Total Revenue = Quantity × Price`  
  - `Delivery Time = Delivery - Order Date`  
- Extracted `Order Month` & `Delivery Hour`

---

## 🔗 Data Modeling (Power Pivot)  
- Linked tables using `Customer ID`, `Order ID`, and `Product ID`  
- Avoided VLOOKUP — used proper relationships  
- Made dashboard faster and dynamic

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features  
- 📌 Total Orders, Total Revenue  
- 🕒 Avg. Delivery Time, Avg. Spend  
- 🛍️ Top Products, Top Cities  
- 📅 Monthly Sales Trend  
- 🔍 Filters by Occasion, City, Month

---

## 🔍 Key Insights  
✅ Sales peak during **Feb, Aug, Nov**  
🎁 **Combo Gifts** and **Personalized Items** sold the most  
📈 Avg. Order Value increased during festivals  
💰 **Anniversaries** & **Raksha Bandhan** gave high revenue

---

## 🎯 Business Value  
📢 Helped the business team to:  
- Plan marketing for festivals  
- Focus on top-performing cities,products  
- Improve delivery efficiency
