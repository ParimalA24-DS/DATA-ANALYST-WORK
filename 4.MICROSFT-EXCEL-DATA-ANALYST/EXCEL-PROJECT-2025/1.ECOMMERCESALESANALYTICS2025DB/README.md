## 📌 Project Overview  
This **E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard** is built using **Excel / Power BI** to analyze sales, profit, orders, and customer trends. The project focuses on **identifying key business insights** to improve profitability and decision-making.  

✔ **Data Cleaning & Preparation using Excel Functions**  
✔ **Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts for Data Analysis**  
✔ **Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth Calculation**  
✔ **Filters for Dynamic Data Exploration**  
✔ **Sales & Profit Analysis across Categories & Regions**  

---

## 🛠️ Data Processing Steps  

### 1️⃣ **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing**  
✅ **Removed duplicates** and handled missing values.  
✅ **Standardized date formats** and categorized data properly.  
✅ **Created lookup tables (LKP)** for mapping **categories, regions, and customer segments**.  

### 2️⃣ **Pivot Tables & Data Analysis**  
📊 **Sales & Profit Analysis**:  
- Created pivot tables for **total sales, profit, and orders**.  
- Used **SUM, AVERAGE, and COUNT** functions to calculate values.  
- Applied conditional formatting to highlight high & low-performing areas.  

📊 **Category-wise Performance**:  
- Compared profits in **Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology** categories.  
- Identified the **highest profit contributor (Technology: $286.4K)**.  

📊 **Region-wise Sales Breakdown**:  
- Used a **geographic heatmap** to show sales variations across states.  
- Identified high-revenue states and potential growth areas.

📌 **Formula Used for YoY Growth:**  
YoY Growth = ((Current Year Value - Previous Year Value) / Previous Year Value) * 100

## 📊 Business Insights & Conclusion  
✔ **Technology leads in profit** – expand product range in this category.  
✔ **Profit Margin Declining** – Need better pricing & cost control.  
✔ **High-Sales Regions Identified** – Invest more in underperforming states.  
✔ **Phones & Chairs** are top-selling subcategories – increase inventory & marketing.
